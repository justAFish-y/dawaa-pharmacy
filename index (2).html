How a request flows

Read

Gemini reads Arabic or English text, or a prescription photo, and pulls out each medicine and quantity.

Match

A fuzzy match finds the closest catalog item. Controlled, out-of-stock or uncertain items go to a pharmacist.

Reserve

Stock is held for two hours. Expired holds return to stock every 15 minutes, and low stock is reported at 22:00.
